## About DayWise

DayWise records income, expenses, accounts, budgets and recurring entries. It does not connect to your bank, transfer money, make payments or provide financial, investment, tax or accounting advice.

## Your records and backups

Check the accuracy of your entries and calculations before relying on them. Keep current JSON backups outside your phone. Local data can be lost when the app is uninstalled, storage is cleared, a device is lost or a restore replaces records. CSV exports cannot be restored as DayWise backups.

## Privacy and security

The DayWise Privacy Policy explains local storage, Android backup and external support. App lock uses Android authentication. DayWise does not separately encrypt its database or exported files. Do not send financial backups or sensitive transactions through support.
